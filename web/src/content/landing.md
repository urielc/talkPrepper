## A tool for preparation, not a substitute for it

Lesson Prep gathers material: the assigned talk, the scriptures and talks it draws on, other conference addresses on the same themes, and questions that might open a discussion. It is a reference desk.

It cannot do the part that matters. The Spirit teaches the teacher first. Study the talk yourself, pray about the people in the room, and let what you gather here serve that preparation rather than replace it. If a summary or a question on these pages ever feels like enough, that is the moment to close the laptop and open the scriptures.

Church leaders have spoken plainly about using tools like this one with care. Their counsel is below.
